# Limitations

No quantum advantage is claimed. Black-Scholes constant volatility is only the baseline. State preparation can dominate costs. Large statevector simulation requires huge HPC memory. Real hardware implementation is limited by circuit depth and noise. Finance usefulness depends on calibration and benchmarking. European option pricing alone is not enough for industry relevance. Portfolio risk and Greeks are the main industry-facing outputs. The local-volatility extension is simplified and should not be overclaimed.
